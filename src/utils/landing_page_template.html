Subterranean Termite Soil Barrier

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about our local pest control treatments in Kochi.

What is the cost of professional pest control in Kochi? +

General residential pest control packages start at ₹999 for apartments. Specialized termite treatments or major rodent proofing/exclusions are quoted after a free inspection.

Are your chemical treatments safe for pets and children? +

Yes. We use CIBRC-registered and WHO-approved odorless green-label chemicals. They are target-specific to pests and low-toxicity to mammals, making them safe for kids and pets once dry.

How long does a typical pest control treatment take? +

A general treatment for cockroaches or ants takes 45 to 60 minutes. Termite Drill-Fill-Seal treatments take 3 to 6 hours depending on structure size.

Do you provide a service warranty? +

Yes, we provide written warranties. Post-construction termite treatments have up to a 5-year warranty, and Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC) have year-round protection.

How often should pest control be done in commercial sites? +

High-risk commercial properties like restaurants, cafeterias, and warehouses should have scheduled monthly inspections and preventative treatments.
