Yes. We use CIBRC-registered and government-approved chemicals that are odorless and target-specific. Our gel bait applications are safe for children and pets because they are applied in hidden cabinet joints where kids cannot reach.

A standard treatment for cockroaches, ants, and spiders in Ernakulam takes between 45 and 60 minutes. Structural termite treatments require drilling and can take 3 to 6 hours.

We use WHO-compliant, government-approved chemicals (such as Fipronil gel baits, Imidacloprid, and synthetic pyrethroids) manufactured by Bayer. These are odorless, highly effective against pests, and safe for indoor use.
